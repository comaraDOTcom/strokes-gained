SG

Practise where the strokes are

Mark Broadie's data on thousands of rounds showed the long game separates handicaps more than putting does. So the plan starts wherever your numbers say the strokes are, which is usually approach play.

RX

Random over blocked

Hitting 50 seven-irons at one flag feels productive and transfers poorly. Motor-learning research favours changing club, target and lie every ball. Every drill here is random practice with a score.

TG

Keep score when you practise

A drill without a pass mark is a warm-up. Each session has a target, and the app logs whether you hit it, so practice gets a trend line just like your rounds.

DM

Decide, then commit

Scott Fawcett's DECADE work and Pia Nilsson's coaching agree on one thing: a committed shot to a sensible target beats a perfect swing at a hopeful one. The Head Game section tracks whether you're doing it.
